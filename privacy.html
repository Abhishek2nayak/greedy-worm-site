Privacy Policy

Greedy Worm: Snake Battle · Last updated: 3 November 2026

Summary

Greedy Worm is played offline against computer players. The game itself does not collect your personal data or send it to us. To keep the game free, it shows ads from Google AdMob, and AdMob collects some device data to show and measure those ads, as described below.

What the game stores on your device

To remember your progress, the game saves the following only on your own phone:

  • the nickname you type in (optional),
  • your chosen skin and sound setting,
  • your coins and game stats (longest length, takedowns, rounds played).

We never receive this data. If you have Android backup turned on, Android may include it in your own device backup with Google, which is controlled by your Google account settings. Uninstalling the game deletes this data.

Ads

The game shows full-screen ads between some rounds, and optional rewarded ads you can choose to watch for a free revive. These ads are provided by Google AdMob. To serve ads, limit how often you see them, measure them and prevent fraud, AdMob may collect and process:

  • your device's advertising ID,
  • your IP address and approximate location derived from it,
  • device information such as model, operating system, language and screen size,
  • how you interact with the ads (for example views and taps), and diagnostic data.

This data is handled by Google under its own policies: how Google uses information from apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy. Data sent to Google is encrypted in transit.

Your choices

  • You can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalised ads, in your phone's Settings (Google › Ads, or Privacy › Ads, depending on your phone).
  • If you are in the European Economic Area, the UK or Switzerland, the game asks for your consent before personalised ads are shown. You can change your choice at any time from Settings › Privacy in the game.
  • You can stop all ad data collection by uninstalling the game.

Permissions

The game uses internet access only so that ads can load. It does not ask for location, camera, microphone, contacts or storage access.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect any information from children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
